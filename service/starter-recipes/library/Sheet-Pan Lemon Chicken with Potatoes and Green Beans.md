# Sheet-Pan Lemon Chicken with Potatoes and Green Beans

**Source:** PantryOS starter
**Servings:** 4
**Published time:** 45 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 lb boneless chicken thighs
- 1 1/2 lb yukon gold potatoes, cut into 1-inch chunks
- 1 lb green beans, trimmed
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 lemon, half juiced and half cut into wedges
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper

## Directions

1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Toss the potatoes with half the olive oil, half the salt, and pepper on a large sheet pan; roast 15 minutes.
2. Meanwhile, toss the chicken with the remaining oil, garlic, oregano, lemon juice, and remaining salt.
3. Push the potatoes to one side, add the chicken to the pan, and roast 15 minutes.
4. Add the green beans, toss briefly with the pan juices, and roast 10 more minutes until the chicken reaches 165°F.
5. Serve with lemon wedges squeezed over everything.
