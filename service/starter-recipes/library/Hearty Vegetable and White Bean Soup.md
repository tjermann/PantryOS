# Hearty Vegetable and White Bean Soup

**Source:** PantryOS starter
**Servings:** 6
**Published time:** 50 minutes

## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 3 carrots, diced
- 3 celery stalks, diced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 can crushed tomatoes
- 2 cans white beans, drained and rinsed
- 6 cups chicken broth
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 bunch kale, stemmed and chopped
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 cup grated parmesan, for serving
- crusty bread, for serving

## Directions

1. Sauté the onion, carrots, and celery in olive oil over medium heat until softened, about 8 minutes. Add garlic and oregano; cook 1 minute.
2. Add the tomatoes, beans, broth, and salt. Simmer 25 minutes.
3. Stir in the kale and simmer 5 minutes until tender.
4. Serve topped with parmesan, with bread alongside.
