# Greek Chicken Bowls with Cucumber and Feta

**Source:** PantryOS starter
**Servings:** 4
**Published time:** 40 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 lb boneless chicken thighs
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 lemon, juiced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 cup jasmine rice
- 1 english cucumber, diced
- 1 pint cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1/2 cup crumbled feta
- 1/4 cup fresh mint, chopped

## Directions

1. Whisk half the olive oil with the lemon juice, garlic, oregano, and salt; marinate the chicken 20 minutes (or all day in the fridge).
2. Cook the rice according to package directions.
3. Sear the chicken in a hot skillet, about 5 minutes per side, until cooked through; rest and slice.
4. Toss the cucumber and tomatoes with the remaining olive oil and a pinch of salt.
5. Build bowls: rice, sliced chicken, cucumber-tomato salad, feta, and mint on top.
