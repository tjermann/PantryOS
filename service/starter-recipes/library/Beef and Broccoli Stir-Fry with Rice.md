# Beef and Broccoli Stir-Fry with Rice

**Source:** PantryOS starter
**Servings:** 4
**Published time:** 35 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1 lb flank steak, thinly sliced against the grain
- 1 lb broccoli, cut into florets
- 1 cup jasmine rice
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp cornstarch
- 1 tbsp honey
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- 3 scallions, sliced

## Directions

1. Cook the rice according to package directions. Whisk the soy sauce, cornstarch, honey, and 1/4 cup water into a sauce.
2. Toss the sliced beef with a spoonful of the sauce and let sit 10 minutes.
3. Stir-fry the broccoli in half the oil over high heat until crisp-tender, about 4 minutes; set aside.
4. Stir-fry the beef in the remaining oil until browned, about 3 minutes. Add garlic and ginger for 30 seconds.
5. Return the broccoli, pour in the sauce, and toss until glossy and thickened. Top with scallions and serve over rice.
