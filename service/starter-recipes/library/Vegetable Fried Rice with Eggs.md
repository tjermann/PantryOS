# Vegetable Fried Rice with Eggs

**Source:** PantryOS starter
**Servings:** 4
**Published time:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 3 cups cooked jasmine rice, preferably day-old
- 4 large eggs, beaten
- 2 carrots, diced small
- 1 cup frozen peas
- 3 scallions, sliced, whites and greens separated
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil

## Directions

1. Scramble the eggs in a splash of the oil in a large skillet; set aside.
2. Stir-fry the carrots and scallion whites in the remaining oil over high heat until the carrots soften, about 4 minutes. Add garlic for 30 seconds.
3. Add the rice, breaking up clumps, and press it into the pan to crisp for 2 minutes.
4. Stir in the peas, soy sauce, sesame oil, and eggs; toss until hot.
5. Top with scallion greens and serve.
