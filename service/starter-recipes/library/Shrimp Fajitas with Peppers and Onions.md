# Shrimp Fajitas with Peppers and Onions

**Source:** PantryOS starter
**Servings:** 4
**Published time:** 30 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 lb large shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 3 bell peppers, sliced
- 1 red onion, sliced
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tsp chili powder
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp salt
- 8 flour tortillas
- 1 avocado, sliced
- 1 lime, cut into wedges
- 1/4 cup cilantro, chopped

## Directions

1. Toss the shrimp with half the oil and half the spices; toss the peppers and onion with the rest.
2. Sear the vegetables in a hot skillet until charred at the edges, about 7 minutes; set aside.
3. Cook the shrimp in the same skillet, about 2 minutes per side.
4. Warm the tortillas. Serve shrimp and vegetables in tortillas with avocado, cilantro, and lime.
