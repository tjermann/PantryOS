# Chickpea and Spinach Coconut Curry

**Source:** PantryOS starter
**Servings:** 4
**Published time:** 30 minutes

## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- 2 tbsp curry powder
- 2 cans chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 1 can crushed tomatoes
- 1 can coconut milk
- 5 oz baby spinach
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 cup jasmine rice
- 1/4 cup cilantro, chopped

## Directions

1. Cook the rice according to package directions.
2. Sauté the onion in olive oil over medium heat until soft, about 5 minutes. Add garlic, ginger, and curry powder; cook 1 minute.
3. Add chickpeas, tomatoes, coconut milk, and salt. Simmer 15 minutes, stirring occasionally.
4. Stir in the spinach until wilted. Serve over rice, topped with cilantro.
