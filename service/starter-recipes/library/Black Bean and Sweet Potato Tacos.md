# Black Bean and Sweet Potato Tacos

**Source:** PantryOS starter
**Servings:** 4
**Published time:** 35 minutes

## Ingredients

- 2 large sweet potatoes, cut into 1/2-inch cubes
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tsp chili powder
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 cans black beans, drained and rinsed
- 8 corn tortillas
- 1 avocado, sliced
- 1/2 cup crumbled feta or cotija cheese
- 1/4 cup cilantro, chopped
- 1 lime, cut into wedges

## Directions

1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Toss the sweet potatoes with olive oil, chili powder, cumin, and salt; roast on a sheet pan 25 minutes until browned.
2. Warm the black beans in a small pot with a splash of water and a pinch of salt.
3. Warm the tortillas in a dry skillet.
4. Fill tortillas with beans and sweet potatoes; top with avocado, cheese, and cilantro. Serve with lime wedges.
