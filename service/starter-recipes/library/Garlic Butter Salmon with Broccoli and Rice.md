# Garlic Butter Salmon with Broccoli and Rice

**Source:** PantryOS starter
**Servings:** 4
**Published time:** 30 minutes

## Ingredients

- 4 salmon fillets
- 1 lb broccoli, cut into florets
- 1 cup jasmine rice
- 3 tbsp butter
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 lemon, half juiced and half in wedges
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper

## Directions

1. Cook the rice according to package directions.
2. Heat the oven to 425°F. Spread the broccoli on a sheet pan, season with salt, and roast 10 minutes.
3. Melt the butter with the garlic; stir in the lemon juice.
4. Push the broccoli aside, add the salmon fillets, brush everything with the garlic butter, and roast 12 minutes until the salmon flakes.
5. Serve over rice with lemon wedges.
