# Turkey and Zucchini Skillet Pasta

**Source:** PantryOS starter
**Servings:** 4
**Published time:** 35 minutes

## Ingredients

- 12 oz penne or rigatoni
- 1 lb ground turkey
- 2 zucchini, quartered lengthwise and sliced
- 1 pint cherry tomatoes, halved
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 cup grated parmesan
- 1/2 cup fresh basil leaves, torn

## Directions

1. Cook the pasta in salted water; reserve 1 cup pasta water before draining.
2. Brown the turkey in olive oil, breaking it up, about 6 minutes. Add garlic, oregano, and pepper flakes for 1 minute.
3. Add the zucchini and tomatoes; cook until the zucchini is tender and the tomatoes burst, about 6 minutes.
4. Toss in the pasta with a splash of pasta water and half the parmesan until saucy.
5. Serve topped with basil and the remaining parmesan.
